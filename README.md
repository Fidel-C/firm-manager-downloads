# Firm Manager: downloads

Law-office management for your own office network: clients, cases, documents, court dates, tasks and billing, with your data kept on your own computer. By @fidelcmedia.

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)**

Firm Manager has two programs. Install the **server** on ONE computer (the central computer, kept on during working hours) and the **client** on every computer where someone works
(including the central one if someone works there).

| Your computer | Server (central computer) | Client (every computer) |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10 / 11 | `FirmManagerServer-Setup-*-windows-x64.exe` | `FirmManager-Setup-*-windows-x64.exe` |
| Debian / Ubuntu | `firmmanager-server_*_amd64.deb` | `firmmanager-client_*_amd64.deb` |

Step by step, with pictures: **[User Guide](USER_GUIDE.md)**.
